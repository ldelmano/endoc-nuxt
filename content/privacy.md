## What is your privacy policy?

At EnDoc, your privacy is especially important to us. Therefore, all personal information taken from our website is kept 100% confidential. As a company, we do not sell, rent or share customer’s information with anyone.

Online shopping requires trust on the part of the consumer. To boost your confidence in our information exchange, EnDoc has built a highly secure and encrypted website with the most advanced technologies here again. This ensures your checkout experience goes as smoothly and safely as possible. To know your information is secure, just look for the ‘HTTPS://’ before the actual web address. This shows you are sending information through a secure website.
<br /><br /><br />

## What information does EnDoc collect from its customers?

To complete an order, we require your personal information including full name, mailing address, email address and credit card information. This info is required by us to verify your payment information, your identity and your mailing address. Not to mention, this information helps us serve you with the personalized attention you deserve. All data taken from you is hosted on a highly secure server that is NOT accessible through the internet.

To better assist you in the future, we do track the way our customers interact with the website. This is purely for internal purposes and does NOT put your secure information at risk.
<br /><br /><br />

## Does EnDoc share my information with other companies?

There are only a few extenuating circumstances when we might share your information with others. For instance, we may provide your info to a third party to verify your credit card information (in order to fulfill and ship the order).

Another reason is if we receive a fraudulent order or transaction. In this case, we report the customer to the proper authorities.\
<br /><br /><br />

## Will EnDoc use my information to contact me?

Only if you choose! When placing an order or visiting our website, you have the opportunity to sign up for our newsletter. From time to time, we may notify you about special offers and other useful information.

At any time, you can elect to not receive promotional offers by clicking ‘Unsubscribe’ at the bottom of the email.
